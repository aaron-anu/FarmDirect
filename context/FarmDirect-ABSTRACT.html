ABSTRACT

 

 

The Digital Farmer Marketplace is an innovative concept that aims to address the issue of reduced farmer earnings and increased consumer prices caused by multiple intermediaries in the agricultural supply chain. The proposed platform directly connects farmers and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) with consumers and bulk buyers, allowing them to sell agricultural products at fair and transparent prices while reducing unnecessary intermediaries. The platform can also provide information about available products, quantities, prices, and delivery options, enabling buyers to make convenient and informed purchasing decisions.

 

The application also offers integrated logistics support to simplify the transportation of agricultural products from farms to buyers. In addition, the system uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) for demand forecasting to predict the future demand for agricultural products based on available market and order data. AI-based route optimization can further help identify efficient transportation routes, reducing delivery time and unnecessary transportation costs.

 

In essence, the proposed marketplace serves to improve farmers' access to markets, increase their earnings, and provide consumers and bulk buyers with agricultural products at more reasonable prices. By reducing unnecessary intermediaries and improving coordination between producers, buyers, and logistics providers, the platform can make the agricultural supply chain more transparent, efficient, and organized. The system can also reduce post-harvest losses by supporting better demand planning and timely transportation of products.

 

The proposed solution will offer an effective, convenient, and technology-driven approach to agricultural marketing. By combining direct farmer-to-buyer connections, logistics management, demand forecasting, and route optimization, the platform can contribute to a more efficient agricultural supply chain while promoting fairer prices for farmers and affordable access to fresh agricultural products for consumers.
