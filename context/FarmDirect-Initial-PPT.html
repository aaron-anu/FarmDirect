FarmDirect

AI-Powered Digital Marketplace for Farmers and Buyers

Aaron Anu Kurien • Abhedh Krishnan J R • Abhinav A • Arjun V S • Ashik Mohammed

The Problem

  • Farmers earn only 33–37% of store prices. Each middleman takes a cut, and farmers don't know what buyers want or what prices look like elsewhere—so they grow blind.
  • India loses 12 million tonnes of vegetables annually after harvest. Tomatoes lose 8%+ on the farm alone because farmers lack demand forecasting and market visibility.
  • Trust and logistics gaps trap both sides. Farmers depend on agents for sales and transport; consumers pay inflated prices. The system leaves farmers underpaid and produce spoiled.

4

Design Thinking, Phase 1: Empathize

Understand the needs and challenges of every user group.

FarmDirect serves three user groups. We studied each group before design.

Farmers and FPOs

We held interviews and surveys. We asked how they sell today, what prices they get, and how hard it is to reach distant markets.

Consumers and Bulk Buyers

We collected feedback on buying habits, prices paid, and the lack of availability and quality information.

Logistics Providers

We asked about transport costs, route inefficiency, and delays that slow delivery.

Define, Phase 2

Refine the problem from the insights of the Empathize stage.

Point-of-View statement

A smallholder farmer, forced to sell through agents at prices he can't question, needs a direct, transparent way to reach buyers because every rupee lost to the chain is a rupee his family doesn't eat.

How Might We

  • HMW connect farmers and buyers directly, without agents?
  • HMW make prices and quantities visible to both sides before a deal?
  • HMW get produce moving fast enough that nothing spoils waiting?

8

Our Vision

FarmDirect eliminates middlemen and creates direct, transparent connections between farmers and buyers—so farmers earn fair prices, buyers access affordable fresh produce, and food reaches markets faster with less waste.
